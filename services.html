How We Work

Our Service Delivery Process

A structured framework that ensures transparency, safety, and quality at every project phase.

STEP 01

Consultation & Site Audit

Our lead engineers conduct a thorough structural and technical assessment of your property site.

  • Detailed site measurement and inspection
  • Structural and technical requirements gathering
  • Initial safety and risk evaluation
STEP 02

Costing & BOQ Estimation

Our Quantity Surveying team prepares an itemized Bill of Quantities with transparent pricing.

  • Itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) preparation
  • Fixed material and labor cost breakdown
  • Project timeline and milestone scheduling
STEP 03

Execution & Supervision

Site engineers mobilize skilled personnel and manage daily execution to technical standards.

  • Active on-site engineering supervision
  • Daily safety briefings and quality checks
  • Weekly progress photo and video updates
STEP 04

Handover & Warranty

Final technical audit, joint client walk-through, official project commissioning, and warranty delivery.

  • Joint client walkthrough and quality sign-off
  • System testing for CCTV, electrical, and doors
  • Official warranty certificate issuance
