Quotation Process

How XASHIMA processes your technical quote from initial audit to project execution.

Step 01

PHASE 01

Initial Requirement & BOQ Analysis

Our senior quantity surveyors review your submission, architectural drawings, and material specifications within 24 hours.

  • Structural drawing review & specification check
  • Material quantity takeoffs & current market index audit
  • Identification of cost saving engineering alternatives
  • Preliminary scope alignment call with project manager

On-Site Technical & Field Audit

A field engineer conducts an physical site inspection in Aba, Abia State to verify measurements, structural conditions, and electrical/water access.

  • Security perimeter & cable routing assessment
