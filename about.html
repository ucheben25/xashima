Corporate Profile

Engineering Excellence & Structural Innovation

XASHIMA is a premier Nigerian multi-disciplinary construction, structural renovation, roofing manufacturing, and electronic security engineering firm serving commercial, industrial, and residential sectors.

Executive Summary

Turnkey Built Environment Execution

Founded on principles of structural integrity and financial transparency, XASHIMA bridges the gap between architectural design and physical execution. From structural foundation engineering and long-span aluminum roofing sheet production to high-security entryways and IP CCTV surveillance, we deliver end-to-end reliability.

  • Proven project execution across Aba, Abia State and surrounding regions
  • In-house roofing sheet manufacturing & steel fabrication capability
  • Experienced engineering and qualified quantity surveying professionals
  • Zero-compromise safety protocols & strict quality control
