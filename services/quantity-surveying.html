Quantity Survey Services

Financial precision, rigorous cost estimation, and comprehensive bill of quantities for capital projects.

Service Overview

Strategic Cost Management & Fiscal Control

XASHIMA delivers detailed quantity surveying and construction cost auditing services. We eliminate financial ambiguity by providing verified bills of quantities, pre-tender estimates, contract procurement guidance, and interim valuation certificates throughout construction.

  • Itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) according to standard measurement rules
  • Feasibility cost modeling and preliminary budget forecasts
  • Contractor tender review, negotiation, and contract advisory
  • On-site valuation of work in progress and variation control
  • Final account reconciliation and comprehensive audit reporting
Key Features

Technical Capabilities

Material Take Offs

Exacting quantity extraction from architectural and structural CAD/BIM blueprints to avoid under or over ordering.

Value Engineering

Identifying alternative high-performance building materials to optimize budget expenditure without compromising quality.

Cost Variance Tracking

Continuous financial auditing to detect price fluctuations, scope drift, and potential budget risks early.

Contractual Compliance

Expert administration of JCT and FIDIC contract terms, payment schedules, and claim evaluations.

Methodology

Execution Process

STEP 01

Consultation

Reviewing client objectives, design concepts, target budgets, and project timeline goals.

STEP 02

Assessment

Measuring structural plans, assessing market rates, labor costs, and site logistics requirements.

STEP 03

Planning

Drafting the itemized Bill of Quantities, cash flow projections, and tender documentation package.

STEP 04

Execution

Managing monthly valuations, approving variation orders, and maintaining strict budget monitoring.

STEP 05

Handover

Final account reconciliation, cost performance report submission, and project financial sign-off.
