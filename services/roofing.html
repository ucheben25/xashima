Roofing Sheet Production, Sales & Installation

Precision manufactured aluminum and step-tile roofing sheets engineered for long-term structural durability.

Service Overview

End-to-End Roofing Solutions for Modern Structures

XASHIMA manages the entire roofing lifecycle, from local coil profiling and custom sheet cutting to high-altitude installation and waterproofing. Our team guarantees leak-proof framing, structural truss integrity, and architectural aesthetics across residential, commercial, and industrial facilities.

  • Precision profiling of long-span aluminum and step-tile sheets
  • Engineered timber and structural steel roof truss fabrication
  • Multi-layer waterproofing membranes and thermal insulation
  • Integrated rainwater collection gutters and downspout systems
  • Comprehensive leak testing, inspection, and multi-year warranties

Key Features

Technical Capabilities

High Grade Alloys

Sourced from certified metal mills ensuring resistance to tropical humidity, UV degradation, and coastal corrosion.

Custom Profiling

On-demand length cutting to exact roof pitch dimensions, minimizing seam overlaps and material waste.

Structural Truss Rigidity

Computerized load calculations for wood and light-gauge steel trusses designed to resist wind uplift forces.

Weatherproof Barrier

Double-sealed valley gutters, flashing details, and self-sealing fasteners preventing water ingress.

Methodology

Execution Process

STEP 01

Consultation

Detailed meeting to define roof geometry, sheet profile options, color choices, and project timelines.

STEP 02

Assessment

On-site structural evaluation, wall alignment verification, and precise laser measurements of roof spans.

STEP 03

Planning

Structural truss engineering calculations, sheet cutting schedules, logistics planning, and safety checks.

STEP 04

Execution

Truss erection, high-altitude sheet fastening with watertight fixings, and flashings installation.

STEP 05

Completion

Water spray pressure testing, structural alignment sign-off, site cleanup, and warranty handover.
