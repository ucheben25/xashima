Security Door Supply & Installation

Reinforced armored steel security doors, biometric access control integration, and vault installations engineered for high-security facilities.

Service Overview

Engineered Physical Perimeter Defense

XASHIMA delivers high-specification security door installations for luxury residences, commercial complexes, banks, and corporate headquarters. Our heavy-duty steel core doors provide forced-entry resistance, ballistic protection, and seamless integration with smart biometric access controls.

  • Heavy-duty gauge galvanized steel core with reinforced internal ribbing
  • Multi-point perimeter locking deadbolts and anti-drill manganese plates
  • Biometric fingerprint scanners, digital PIN keypads, and RFID readers
  • Bulletproof composite core options for high-risk access points
  • Reinforced frame anchoring with structural concrete expansion bolts

Key Features

Technical Capabilities

Multi-Point Locking

Simultaneous 12 to 18-point deadbolt locking mechanism extending into top, bottom, and side frame masonry.

Biometric Integration

Commercial-grade fingerprint, facial recognition, and encrypted NFC card access integrated directly into door trims.

Ballistic & Fire Rating

Fire-rated composite insulation offering thermal barrier protection and optional ballistic resistance classifications.

Reinforced Sub-Frames

Heavy-duty 2.5mm tubular steel sub-frames deep-anchored directly into reinforced concrete wall pillars.

Methodology

Execution Process

STEP 01

Site Survey

Laser measurement of structural wall openings, wall load-bearing capacity, and electrical conduit routing.

STEP 02

Specification

Selection of steel gauge, lock classification, decorative architectural finish, and access control hardware.

STEP 03

Sub-Frame Anchoring

Precision leveling and heavy bolt anchoring of the structural steel sub-frame with reinforced mortar filling.

STEP 04

Door Leaf Hanging

Mounting heavy-duty ball-bearing hinges, adjusting multi-point lock alignment, and connecting power feeds.

STEP 05

Testing & Handover

Enrolling biometric credentials, testing emergency release overrides, and providing master keys and warranty.
