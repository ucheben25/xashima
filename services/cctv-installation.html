CCTV Camera Installation & Surveillance

Enterprise surveillance networks, high-resolution IP cameras, and remote monitoring configurations tailored for complete facility oversight.

Service Overview

24/7 Digital Security & Monitoring

XASHIMA designs and implements state-of-the-art IP CCTV surveillance networks that provide uncompromising visibility and security. From single-site commercial installations to multi-campus industrial monitoring, our systems are built for reliability, clear imagery, and seamless remote access.

  • High-resolution 4K and 1080p IP camera deployment
  • Network Video Recorder (NVR) and server configuration
  • Concealed structured cabling and fiber optic transmission
  • Mobile application integration for remote viewing
  • Preventive maintenance and system upgrades

Methodology

Execution Process

STEP 01

Site Survey

Evaluating the premises to identify blind spots, critical entry points, and network requirements.

STEP 02

System Design

Mapping camera locations, selecting hardware, and designing the storage architecture.

STEP 03

Cabling & Routing

Running shielded CAT6 cables neatly and securely to all designated camera positions.

STEP 04

Mounting & Setup

Mounting cameras, configuring the NVR system, setting angles, and testing night vision.

STEP 05

Client Handover

Configuring mobile remote apps, training client staff on playback, and providing support.
