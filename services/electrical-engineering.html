Electrical Engineering & Installations

Safe, compliant, and scalable electrical power distribution and architectural lighting design for modern facilities.

Service Overview

Reliable Power Distribution Systems

XASHIMA's electrical engineering division manages the full lifecycle of power distribution. From initial load calculations and circuit design to the final installation of distribution boards and custom lighting fixtures, our certified engineers ensure flawless execution that meets strict safety protocols.

  • Electrical load balancing and circuit design
  • Concealed PVC and galvanized conduit piping
  • Main distribution board (MDB) configuration
  • Backup generator and inverter integration
  • Interior and exterior architectural lighting

Key Features

Technical Capabilities

Load Balancing

Calculating exact power demands to properly size cables, breakers, and prevent overloading or thermal faults.

Safety Compliance

Executing all electrical wiring according to current international and Nigerian engineering safety standards.

Energy Efficiency

Recommending and installing low-consumption LED lighting and automated motion-sensing circuits.

Backup Integration

Seamlessly integrating changeover switches, diesel generators, and solar inverter battery banks.

Methodology

Execution Process

STEP 01

Load Analysis

Assessing the facility's equipment and lighting to determine the total electrical power requirements.

STEP 02

Drafting

Creating technical drawings for conduit routing, socket locations, and panel placements.

STEP 03

Conduit & Wiring

Chasing walls, laying concealed conduit pipes, and pulling high-grade copper cables.

STEP 04

Panel Dressing

Installing distribution boards, circuit breakers, and terminating all structural wiring securely.

STEP 05

Testing

Performing continuity, insulation resistance, and earthing tests before final commissioning.
