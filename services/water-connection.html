Water Connector Installation & Hydraulic Plumbing

Heavy-duty water connector installations, municipal main tie-ins, and high-pressure hydraulic plumbing networks.

Service Overview

Reliable Hydraulic Networks & Metered Connections

XASHIMA designs and installs robust water distribution networks for multi-story residential towers, commercial complexes, and industrial sites. We specialize in electrofusion HDPE jointing, PPR riser systems, high-capacity water connector installations, pressure booster pumps, and water meter assemblies.

  • High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) and PPR main line installation
  • State water board main line tie-in and commercial meter connection
  • Multistage booster pump sets with pressure vessel configuration
  • Underground suction tanks and overhead storage distribution plumbing
  • Rigorous hydrostatic pressure testing and chlorination line flushing
Key Features

Technical Capabilities

Fusion Jointing

Thermofusion and electrofusion welded joints delivering 100% leak-proof performance under high working pressure.

Automated Boosting

Variable speed inverter pumps maintaining constant water pressure across upper floors without surge shocks.

Filtration Integration

Multi-stage sediment strainers, iron removal filters, and water softeners protecting fixtures and boilers.

Regulatory Compliance

Connections planned and installed in strict compliance with municipal water utility engineering codes.

Methodology

Execution Process

STEP 01

Consultation

Evaluating target water flow rates, source availability, storage capacity, and plumbing fixture counts.

STEP 02

Assessment

Hydraulic head loss calculation, soil trenching survey, and municipal main line pressure check.

STEP 03

Planning

Piping layout schematics, pipe diameter sizing, pump specification selection, and utility permit approval.

STEP 04

Execution

Trench digging, pipe fusion, main connector installation, pump mounting, and reservoir connection.

STEP 05

Handover

Hydrostatic pressure hold test (1.5x working load), chemical line disinfection, flow verification, and sign-off.
