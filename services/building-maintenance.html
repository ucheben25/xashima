Building Maintenance & Facility Upkeep

Comprehensive facility management, structural integrity preservation, and rapid technical maintenance.

Service Overview

Protecting Asset Value & Structural Lifespan

XASHIMA offers end-to-end building maintenance and facility upkeep contracts designed to preserve structural integrity, maintain utility efficiency, and protect property market value. We handle routine structural audits, MEP servicing, waterproofing fixes, and emergency response across Aba, Abia State, and beyond.

  • Routine structural integrity audits and thermal scanning
  • Preventive MEP servicing (electrical panels, pumps, HVAC piping)
  • Concrete repair, epoxy injection, and anti-damp treatments
  • Facade sealant renewal, exterior painting, and roofing repairs
  • 24/7 rapid emergency repair dispatch for critical facility outages
Key Features

Technical Capabilities

Preventive Audits

Scheduled diagnostic inspections identifying hairline cracks, moisture ingress, and electrical overloads before failures occur.

Structural Remediation

Specialized resin injection, carbon fiber reinforcement, and concrete patch repairs restoring load bearing capacity.

Lifecycle Extension

Regular maintenance of pumps, distribution boards, and roof coats extending operational life and lowering capital expenditure.

Rapid Response SLA

Guaranteed response times for commercial emergency callouts involving plumbing bursts, power trips, or storm damage.

Methodology

Execution Process

STEP 01

Consultation

Initial walkthrough with facility managers to evaluate building age, operational demands, and service history.

STEP 02

Assessment

Comprehensive structural and MEP audit to compile a baseline condition asset register and repair log.

STEP 03

Planning

Formulating a custom Service Level Agreement (SLA), preventive maintenance calendar, and spare parts inventory.

STEP 04

Execution

Performing scheduled maintenance, structural fixes, component overhauls, and surface restoration works.

STEP 05

Handover

Detailed inspection report, digital asset maintenance log update, and formal quarterly review sign-off.
