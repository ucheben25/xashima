Cleaning & Fumigation Services

Professional residential, commercial, and industrial deep cleaning, post-construction cleanup, thermal fogging, and pest control operations.

Service Overview

Comprehensive Environmental Hygiene & Pest Management

Under the leadership of Mr. Nwaojimuo Innocent Eze (Head of Supervision & Head of Cleaning & Fumigation Services), XASHIMA provides integrated sanitation, decontamination, and environmental cleaning solutions for residential estates, corporate facilities, commercial plazas, and industrial warehouses.

  • Post-construction deep cleaning, cement haze removal, and surface polishing
  • Commercial office, plaza, and institutional scheduled sanitation
  • Residential move-in, move-out, and routine deep sanitization
  • Targeted thermal fogging and residual spraying for pest control
  • Safe handling with personal protective equipment (PPE) and environmental care

Core Service Areas

Comprehensive Sanitation Capabilities

Residential Deep Cleaning

Comprehensive home deep cleaning, tile scrubbing, window detailing, kitchen degreasing, and sanitary bathroom disinfection.

Commercial & Office Cleaning

Routine and contract janitorial upkeep for corporate offices, banking halls, retail centers, and multi-tenant commercial plazas.

Our Process

Structured 4-Step Execution

STEP 01

Site Assessment

Our supervisors inspect the site to assess contamination levels, infestation vectors, surface types, and square footage.

STEP 02

Method & Chemical Selection

We specify appropriate eco-friendly detergents, industrial scrubbing machinery, or certified pest eradication formulations.

STEP 03

Rigorous Execution

Equipped personnel execute deep cleaning, high-pressure washing, mechanical scrubbing, and precision fogging safely.

STEP 04

Quality Sign-Off

Final inspection walk-through with client, re-entry safety clearance certification, and ongoing maintenance scheduling.
