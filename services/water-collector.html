Water Collector Installation & Hydraulic Plumbing

Heavy-duty water collector installations, rainwater drainage gutters, cistern hookups, and high-pressure hydraulic plumbing networks.

Service Overview

Reliable Water Collector Systems & Hydraulic Engineering

XASHIMA designs and installs engineered water collector networks and hydraulic plumbing systems for commercial complexes, industrial facilities, and residential estates. We specialize in precision roof rainwater collector gutters, high-flow downpipes, underground cistern connections, booster pump stations, and municipal water line hookups.

Our solutions prevent foundation erosion, channel high-volume storm runoff effectively, and integrate sustainable water harvesting capabilities with full pressure testing and thorough quality checks.

  • Heavy-duty seamless aluminum and PVC water collector gutter systems
  • High-capacity storm runoff drainage channels and architectural downpipes
  • Underground cistern, storage tank, and borehole pressure pump integration
  • High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) and PPR distribution plumbing
  • Hydrostatic pressure testing, leak prevention, and flow rate calibration

Key Features

Technical Capabilities

High-Capacity Collector Gutters

Heavy-duty perimeter box gutters sized specifically for tropical rain intensities to eliminate roof overflow and wall staining.

Fusion Jointing & HDPE

Thermofusion and electrofusion welded joints delivering 100% leak-proof performance under high working pressure.

Automated Pressure Boosting

Variable speed inverter pumps maintaining constant water pressure across upper floors without hydraulic shock.

Sediment Filtration

Multi-stage leaf guards, sediment strainers, and filtration setups protecting storage tanks and plumbing fixtures.

Methodology

Execution Process

STEP 01

Consultation

Evaluating roof catchment area, target water volume, storage capacity, and plumbing requirements.

STEP 02

Assessment

Hydraulic slope calculation, gutter bracket spacing verification, and drainage path layout.

STEP 03

Fabrication

Custom water collector gutter forming, downpipe sizing, and bracket fabrication for exact fascia alignment.

STEP 04

Installation

Gutter mounting, downpipe fitting, collector filter hookup, storage tank piping, and pump integration.

STEP 05

Handover

Hydrostatic drainage flow test, pressure testing, quality sign-off, and formal project completion documentation.
