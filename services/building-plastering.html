Plastering & Finishing Services

Precision wall plastering, screeding, and architectural coating for perfectly leveled and pristine building surfaces.

Service Overview

Flawless Surface Preparation

The foundation of a premium building aesthetic begins with perfectly rendered surfaces. XASHIMA provides expert plastering, screeding, and architectural rendering services that seal blockwork, prevent moisture ingress, and create flawlessly smooth canvases for final paint finishes.

  • Interior and exterior blockwork plastering
  • High-precision floor screeding and leveling
  • Damp-proof course (DPC) integration
  • Decorative stucco and textured exterior rendering
  • Crack repair and structural surface preparation
Key Features

Technical Capabilities

Precision Leveling

Utilizing laser-guided straight edges and plumb lines to guarantee geometrically perfect wall and floor surfaces.

Weather Resistance

Applying specialized water-resistant external renders designed to withstand tropical downpours and humidity.

Crack Prevention

Embedding fiberglass mesh in high-stress structural joints to prevent hairline fractures as the building settles.

Material Quality

Sourcing optimal sand-to-cement ratios and premium binding additives to ensure maximum adhesion strength.

Methodology

Execution Process

STEP 01

Surface Prep

Cleaning blockwork, hacking smooth concrete, and applying bonding agents to ensure adhesion.

STEP 02

Beading

Fixing metal angle beads and mesh strips across joints to create sharp, protected architectural corners.

STEP 03

Rendering

Applying the primary cement-sand mixture in controlled layers to build up a perfectly plumb surface.

STEP 04

Floating

Using straight edges and wooden floats to level the render and eliminate waves or depressions.

STEP 05

Finishing

Troweling the final coat for a glass-smooth finish, ready for painting or POP installations.
