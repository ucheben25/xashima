Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

At XASHIMA Engineering, we collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you request a project quote, fill out a contact form, or communicate with our staff. This information may include your name, email address, phone number, physical address, and details regarding your prospective construction or renovation projects.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to understand your project needs, formulate accurate cost estimates (Bills of Quantities), manage our business relationship with you, and communicate project updates. We may also use your contact details to send you relevant information about our services, although you may opt out of these communications at any time.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share necessary details with trusted subcontractors and partners strictly for the purpose of executing your project, under strict confidentiality agreements. We may also disclose information when legally required to do so by the authorities of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance user experience, analyze site traffic, and understand where our visitors are coming from. You can control the use of cookies at the individual browser level.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, update, or request the deletion of your personal information held by XASHIMA Engineering. If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.
